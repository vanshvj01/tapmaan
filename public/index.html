Heatwave monitoring

Twenty-five stations across seven IMD regions, classified against official heatwave criteria. Departure from the local normal decides the warning, not the raw temperature.

Station positions by latitude and longitude. Select a station to open its forecast.

Reading the classification

IMD does not declare a heatwave on a single number. A station qualifies once its maximum passes a base threshold — 40°C in the plains, 37°C on the coast, 30°C in the hills — and then the departure from that station's own normal decides severity.

Normal Heat watch, departure 3.0 to 4.4°C Heatwave, departure 4.5 to 6.4°C Severe, departure 6.5°C or more

An absolute maximum of 45°C triggers a heatwave and 47°C a severe heatwave, whatever the departure.
